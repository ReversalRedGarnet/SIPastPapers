# Rights and permissions

This note explains plainly how SIPastPapers handles the rights to the exam
papers it hosts. It is a statement of practice, not legal advice.

## What the project is

An independent, student-run archive of **historical** Solomon Islands national
exam papers. It is not run by, affiliated with or endorsed by the Solomon
Islands Government, the Ministry of Education (MEHRD) or any exam authority.
It never hosts unreleased or current exam material.

## What is licensed and what is not

- **Code**: MIT licensed (see `LICENSE`).
- **Exam papers**: not covered by that licence. Each paper has its own rights
  record in the database. The code licence says nothing about who owns the
  exams.

## How a paper gets published

1. A paper is ingested. Its rights record is **always created as
   "pending institutional approval"**, whatever flags are used.
2. A person records the basis for publishing it: `teacher-verified`,
   `personal-collection`, `institutional-submission` or `other`.
3. A paper can only be published once the basis, the approver and the evidence
   are filled in. The tool refuses otherwise.
4. Most papers are currently published on a **teacher-verified** basis: a
   network of teachers, education officers or former students confirmed the
   paper's authenticity.

## What this does not mean

A completed rights record means a named person vouched for a paper. It does
**not** mean MEHRD or any institution approved it. [Update this paragraph if
MEHRD has since been approached: state the date and outcome.]

## Corrections and takedown

Anyone can report a problem from the link on every paper page. A report that
plausibly concerns ownership, privacy, security or authenticity puts the paper
on rights hold while it is reviewed. The outcome (kept, corrected, replaced,
restricted or withdrawn) is logged. Unpublishing a paper stops its download
links straight away.
